Questions partners ask

When do I get paid?

On the 15th, for the month before. Balances under $50 roll forward so you're not chasing a $4 transfer.

What counts as a conversion?

A referred account that starts paying. We record every touch, and pay on the last one before signup.

How long is the attribution window?

30 days from the visit to your page. We also stamp the referral onto the account at signup, so it survives cleared cookies and device switches.

What if someone refunds or churns?

Commission is reversed for refunds inside the refund window. Ordinary churn just ends future commission — nothing is clawed back.

Can I refer my own company?

No — self-referrals don't earn commission. You get a free Agency account anyway.

Do I need an audience to qualify?

Not a big one. We care more about whether the people who trust you actually run campaigns.

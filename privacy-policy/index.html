1. Who We Are

UTMFOX is a business-to-business software platform that helps businesses and teams create, standardize, manage, govern, analyze, and deploy UTM-tagged URLs, campaign links, vanity URLs, and redirects.

For information UTMFOX collects directly for its own business purposes, such as account information, website analytics, billing information, and support communications, UTMFOX generally determines how and why that information is processed.

When UTMFOX processes information through the Service on behalf of a Customer, UTMFOX may act as a service provider or processor and process that information according to the Customer's instructions, applicable agreements, and applicable law.

2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with UTMFOX, the features you use, and the integrations you enable.

Account Information

When you create or use a UTMFOX account, we may collect:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • company or organization name;
  • account username or identifier;
  • team membership;

  • subscription plan;
  • account preferences;
  • login history; and
  • other information you voluntarily provide.

When you subscribe to a paid UTMFOX plan, we or our payment processing providers may collect information necessary to process payment, such as:

3. Incidental Personal Information

UTMFOX is not designed as a customer relationship management system, consumer database, or system for storing large quantities of personally identifiable consumer information.

However, personal information may occasionally appear incidentally in:

  • URL parameters;
  • analytics information;

  • Cloudflare information;
  • logs;
  • support messages; or
  • information supplied by a Customer.

Customers should avoid placing sensitive personal information, passwords, financial information, health information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information into UTM parameters or URLs.

Customers are responsible for ensuring that their use of UTMFOX complies with applicable privacy laws and their own privacy obligations.

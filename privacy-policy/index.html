1. Who We Are

UTMFOX is a business-to-business software platform that helps businesses and teams create, standardize, manage, govern, analyze, and deploy UTM-tagged URLs, campaign links, vanity URLs, and redirects.

For information UTMFOX collects directly for its own business purposes, such as account information, website analytics, billing information, and support communications, UTMFOX generally determines how and why that information is processed.

When UTMFOX processes information through the Service on behalf of a Customer, UTMFOX may act as a service provider or processor and process that information according to the Customer's instructions, applicable agreements, and applicable law.

9. Customer-Authorized Google Analytics 4 OAuth Access

UTMFOX offers an optional attribution integration that allows a Customer to connect the Customer's own Google Analytics 4 ("GA4") property to UTMFOX.

This integration is separate from analytics that UTMFOX may use on its own website or application.

Customer Authorization

UTMFOX does not access a Customer's GA4 property unless an authorized Customer user affirmatively chooses to connect Google Analytics and completes Google's OAuth authorization process.

The Customer controls whether to enable this integration.

OAuth Permission Requested

For the attribution integration, UTMFOX requests read-only Google Analytics access using the OAuth scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/analytics.readonly

This permission allows UTMFOX to read Google Analytics reporting data available to the authorized Customer account.

UTMFOX does not use this scope to edit the Customer's GA4 property, change analytics settings, modify events, delete analytics information, manage users, or make other changes to the Customer's Google Analytics account.

Why UTMFOX Requests GA4 Access

UTMFOX accesses the Customer's GA4 reporting data to provide customer-facing attribution functionality.

Specifically, UTMFOX uses GA4 reporting data to compare traffic recorded in Google Analytics against campaigns and UTM-tagged links issued or managed through UTMFOX.

This allows UTMFOX to provide features such as the Attribution Drift Dashboard, which is designed to help Customers identify issues including:

  • campaign naming inconsistencies;
  • unexpected attribution;
  • missing or altered UTM values;
  • differences between issued UTM campaigns and GA4 reporting;
  • campaign traffic appearing under unexpected sources or mediums; and
  • other attribution discrepancies.

UTMFOX accesses GA4 information only for providing or improving these customer-facing analytics and attribution features and for related security or support purposes permitted by applicable policy and law.

Google Analytics Data Accessed

UTMFOX requests only the Google Analytics reporting information reasonably necessary to provide the connected attribution features.

This may include aggregated reporting information associated with:

  • sessions;
  • campaigns;
  • traffic sources;
  • traffic mediums;
  • UTM campaign values;
  • reporting dates;
  • campaign performance; and
  • related aggregate analytics metrics and dimensions necessary to reconcile UTMFOX campaign records with GA4 reporting.

UTMFOX does not intentionally use this integration to retrieve or build profiles containing raw user-level browsing histories or personally identifiable profiles of individual website visitors.

What UTMFOX Stores

UTMFOX stores only the information reasonably necessary to provide the attribution functionality and historical reporting associated with the Customer's UTMFOX account.

UTMFOX stores session and campaign-level aggregate information derived from the authorized GA4 property.

UTMFOX does not intentionally store raw user-level Google Analytics event streams for this feature.

UTMFOX may also securely store OAuth authorization information, including access or refresh tokens, as necessary to maintain the Customer-authorized connection to Google Analytics.

OAuth tokens and credentials are treated as authentication information and are not used for analytics, advertising, artificial-intelligence training, or unrelated purposes.

How Long GA4 Information Is Retained

Aggregated GA4 session and campaign information may be retained for the duration of the Customer's active UTMFOX account so that UTMFOX can provide historical attribution reporting and comparisons.

A Customer may request deletion of stored information associated with its account, subject to applicable legal, security, backup, and legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

If a Customer closes or deletes its UTMFOX account, GA4-derived information associated with that account will be deleted or de-identified in accordance with UTMFOX's general data-retention procedures described in this Privacy Policy.

Temporary backup copies may remain for a limited period as part of routine backup and disaster-recovery processes before being overwritten or deleted.

Disconnecting Google Analytics

Connecting Google Analytics is optional.

A Customer may disconnect its Google Analytics integration at any time using the Disconnect control provided within the UTMFOX application.

After the Customer disconnects the integration:

  • UTMFOX stops requesting new GA4 reporting data through that authorization;
  • the stored OAuth authorization used by UTMFOX to maintain the integration is removed, invalidated, or otherwise made unavailable for continued UTMFOX access;
  • the Attribution Drift Dashboard will no longer receive new information from the disconnected GA4 property; and
  • previously stored session and campaign aggregates may remain as historical UTMFOX account records unless the Customer requests deletion or deletes the applicable account.

Disconnecting Google Analytics does not delete or modify information stored within the Customer's Google Analytics property.

Sharing and Transfer of Google Analytics Data

UTMFOX does not sell Customer-authorized Google Analytics data.

UTMFOX does not transfer Customer-authorized Google Analytics data to data brokers.

UTMFOX does not use Customer-authorized Google Analytics data for:

  • cross-site behavioral advertising;
  • personalized advertising;
  • advertising audiences;
  • retargeting;
  • unrelated marketing;
  • determining creditworthiness;
  • generalized AI model training; or
  • purposes unrelated to the customer-facing functionality for which access was granted.

UTMFOX may permit service providers or infrastructure providers to process Google Analytics information only where reasonably necessary to operate, secure, maintain, or provide the authorized UTMFOX functionality and subject to appropriate contractual and security requirements.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to investigate security incidents or abuse.

Google API Services User Data Policy

UTMFOX's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

UTMFOX limits its use of Google Analytics information obtained through OAuth to the purposes disclosed in this Privacy Policy and the customer-facing functionality for which the Customer authorized access.

Questions about billing

What counts as a tracked session?

A visit that arrives through a UTM Fox–governed campaign link and is reconciled against your connected GA4 property. Traffic to your site outside a tracked campaign doesn't count.

What happens if I exceed a limit?

Nothing breaks. You'll see a notice in-app and we'll ask you to upgrade — we don't stop redirects that are already live or delete campaigns.

Do you charge per user?

No. Seats are capped per plan but the price is flat per organization. Adding a teammate on Growth costs nothing until you pass ten.

Is there a free trial on paid plans?

Yes, and there's also a genuinely usable free tier — you don't have to start a clock to evaluate the governance engine.

What happens to my redirects if I downgrade?

Redirects over the new limit stay live in your Cloudflare account; you just can't create more until you're back under the cap. We never delete a rule from your infrastructure.

Can I pay annually or by invoice?

Annual billing is available on every paid tier. Invoicing and purchase orders are available on Business and Agency.

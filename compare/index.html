Before you switch

The questions that come up every time

Can I bring the links I already have?

Existing links keep working — nothing you've published depends on us. Campaigns are recreated in UTM Fox so they're governed and scored going forward. A bulk CSV importer is not built yet; for large libraries, talk to us.

What happens to my links if I cancel?

Redirects created in your own Cloudflare account stay in your Cloudflare account. Short links on our shared utm.wiki domain stop resolving — those are a convenience, not a dependency.

Do I need Cloudflare to use UTM Fox?

No. Governance, validation, health scoring, and campaign history all work without connecting anything. Cloudflare is required only for deploying vanity redirects on your own domain.

How long does setup take?

Governance rules and your first campaign: minutes. Connecting Cloudflare: one OAuth screen. There is no tag to install and no script on your site.

Is my traffic data shared with UTM Fox?

Only what you connect. GA4 attribution monitoring reads the property you authorize. We never sit in the click path, so we have no independent record of your visitors.

What if my team is already on a spreadsheet?

That's the most common starting point, and honestly the easiest migration — paste the sheet in, and every inconsistency you've accumulated shows up as a score.
